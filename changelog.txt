pair_recon Driver-side window statistics, Right log capture, and row-level differences — 2026-10-29

Summary
- Right window statistics of a window with at most `RIGHT_STATS_DRIVER_ROWS` (2000000) sampled rows are projected by Spark as normalized text and reduced in pandas on the driver; larger windows keep the batched Spark aggregates.
- Spark queries become plain projections sized by `RIGHT_STATS_DRIVER_CELLS` (50000000 cells per scan), so a few-thousand-row window of 867 columns is fetched in one scan instead of 18, and the planner handles them in seconds.
- Each distinct text is hashed once (sha256 first 16 hex digits weighted by its count), matching the Left DuckDB and Spark aggregate results exactly.
- After comparison the Right uploads every sampled row of each differing window for its differing columns (`rows.csv.gz`: normalized key, raw and normalized values); the Left joins them with its local window CSV in DuckDB on the normalized key and writes the first `sample_rows` row-level differences (different, left only, right only) as `differences/<vintage>/rows.xlsx` on the Volume and beside `left.csv` locally.
- The Right notebook buffers its log in memory and rewrites `_protocol/right.log` on the Volume every 30 seconds from a daemon thread (20 MB cap, failed writes retried, never raised into the job); the Left copies it to `right.log` beside `left.log` once the job ends.
